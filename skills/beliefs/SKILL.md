# beliefs — the belief store index

The stage-one index of the belief store: the world-facing claims a diagnosis
leans on — how a dependency behaves under load, what an environment defaults to,
how a failure propagates — held as priced hypotheses, one ledger entry per claim
(`corpus/beliefs/`, a ledger — see
[/corpus/beliefs/README.md](/corpus/beliefs/README.md)). Each claim freezes with
its falsifier, deadline, and reference at admission.

## The three-stage read

1. **Skill name and description** — always visible; the legible key that routes
   a session to open this skill.
2. **This index** — one line per claim: the hook (`consult-when`), the deadline,
   the status, a `[postdiction]` marker where the claim was born settled, and a
   `superseded-by` pointer where one applies. The claim sentence is never here.
   The region below is generated by `tools/index-gen`; edit the claims in
   `corpus/beliefs/`, never this region.
3. **The belief record** — `corpus/beliefs/B-nnnn.md`, opened when the work
   depends on the claimed fact.

## Read protocol

The hook is `consult-when` — the work shape whose sessions depend on the claim.
It keys on how the work presents, not on the claim's content: consultation keys
on work-shape, falsification on world-state, and the two rarely share a word.

`consult-when` is written in the tenant's grown work-shape vocabulary — the terms
the tenant names for the work shapes that recur (see
[/corpus/vocabulary/README.md](/corpus/vocabulary/README.md)). A consult fires
when the work in hand carries one of those shapes.

**Do:** ground the shape of the work in hand in the shared vocabulary before
scanning `consult-when`.
**Don't:** don't match `consult-when` on surface words alone — a shape named in
the vocabulary is the join key, and an ad-hoc paraphrase slides past the fire.

**Do:** consult a claim whose `consult-when` matches the dependence in the work
in hand.
**Don't:** don't read the claim from the index — the line carries `consult-when`,
never the claim; the assertion and its falsifier live in the record.

**Do:** follow a `superseded → B-nnnn` pointer to the narrowed successor and
consult that record.
**Don't:** don't skip a `settled` or `superseded` entry a pointer led you to — a
retired claim stays listed as the record of what was believed, at what price.

**Do:** treat a `[postdiction]` entry as a transcribed import.
**Don't:** don't count a `[postdiction]` claim as evidence of skill — a claim
born settled at zero price is excluded from every skill measure.
