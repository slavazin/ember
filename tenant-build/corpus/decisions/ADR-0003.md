# The shipped constitution holds ten articles; the twelve-article set stays an inward register

## Context
Two constitutions carry different counts under one word. The shipped operating law is the
always-loaded tier, whose cost is paid every session; the inward build register carries
three hackathon-meta articles (the Qodo-PR gate, the demo-arc, the Bright-Data latch) that
no deployed tenant needs.

## Options considered
### Option A — one twelve-article constitution, shipped
Shape: the meta articles ride the always-loaded tier. Verdict: rejected — the meta articles
cost every session and carry no tenant duty.
### Option B — a ten-article shipped skill, a twelve-article inward register
Shape: the shipped skill drops the three meta articles; the inward register keeps them.
Verdict: chosen.

## Decision
The shipped constitution skill holds at most ten articles, enforced mechanically; the
twelve-article constitution is an inward build register that never enters the layer.

## Consequences
Keeps the always-loaded tier small and tenant-agnostic. Forecloses shipping a
hackathon-meta article in the layer.

## Warrant
- The lint caps the shipped skill at ten articles (as of PR #6, tools/corpus-lint.ts
  `constitutionCap`); the shipped skill holds nine (as of PR #4, skills/constitution/SKILL.md).
- False if: an eleventh article earns the always-loaded tier, forcing an eviction.
