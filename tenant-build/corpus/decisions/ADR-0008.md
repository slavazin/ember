# Entry-file selection is pinned once in a shared predicate; no driver keeps its own filename literal

## Context
The producer and the verifier both select a store's entry files. When each derives the
admitted-entry filename shape on its own, the two matchers drift — the discovery rule
reopened twice, first on prefix-and-digits, then on the digit count.

## Options considered
### Option A — each driver carries its own filename regex
Shape: index-gen and corpus-lint each match `<prefix>-<digits>`. Verdict: rejected — two
literals drift apart in silence.
### Option B — one shared predicate, imported
Shape: the contract module exports the selector; every driver imports it. Verdict: chosen.

## Decision
The admitted-entry filename test is defined once, in the shared contract module, and
imported by every driver that selects entry files; no driver carries its own filename regex.

## Consequences
The producer and verifier resolve one identical entry set. Forecloses two matchers drifting,
and makes a change to the filename shape a one-file change.

## Warrant
- The contract module exports `isEntryFile` and `STORE_PREFIX`, imported by index-gen and
  corpus-lint (as of PR #8, tools/index-contract.ts).
- False if: a driver needs an entry-selection rule the shared predicate cannot express.
