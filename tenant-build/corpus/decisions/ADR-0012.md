# A lens is a guiding question paired with its counterfactual; the retro is a set of lenses

## Context
The close-out's reflection needs a shape the slow loop can adjudicate. A bare prompt —
"what did the run miss?" — invites session-bias narration and manufactures candidates
for genuine novelty that no entry could have caught. The corpus already runs on bounded
directives: LANGUAGE.md's L3 requires every directive to ship with the counter-directive
naming its overshoot. The reflection wants the same discipline, and the construct
recurs beyond the close-out — a recon subagent's investigation shapes are the same kind
of open, angle-selecting question.

## Options considered
### Option A — freeform retro prose
Shape: an open reflection written into the pull request. Verdict: rejected — unbounded
reflection produces slop candidates the adjudicator must clear, and drifts into
narration of what the session did.
### Option B — reflection as counterfactual-paired lenses
Shape: each angle ships with the challenge that filters its answer, isomorphic to L3.
Verdict: chosen — the counterfactual is the gate an answer passes before it becomes a
candidate.

## Decision
A lens is a guiding question paired with its counterfactual — an angle on the work plus
the challenge that keeps the angle's answer honest. The close-out's retro walks a fixed
set of lenses; a lens answer becomes a candidate only when its counterfactual is
answered too. `lens` is a lexicon term and its authoring rule is the Lens language in
`corpus/LANGUAGE.md`; the construct is reusable beyond the close-out.

## Consequences
Enables filtering a reflection answer before it reaches the slow loop as a candidate.
Forecloses a retro that files a lens answer unable to survive its own counterfactual,
and forecloses shipping a lens without its counterfactual.

## Warrant
- The `lens` term and the Lens language live in `corpus/LANGUAGE.md`; the retro lens set
  lives in `skills/close/SKILL.md` (as of 2026-08-28).
- False if: the retro ships a lens without a counterfactual, or the lens construct is
  removed from the authoring language.
