# The slow loop's agent-drafting pass is its own skill, consolidate, reading fast-loop pull requests; promotion-review stays the human's adjudication ladder

## Context
The slow loop has two parties by Article 3: an agent that reads accumulated
signal and drafts promotions, and the human who routes, adjudicates, and merges.
The `promotion-review` skill carried only the human's half — the graded bars, the
slot-signature table, the route-before-mint ladder — with no procedure for the
agent-side act the doctrine's consolidation pass requires: reading across the
fast-loop pull requests, walking lenses over their dispositions and surviving lens
answers, and drafting the residue. Folding that agent procedure into
`promotion-review` would put the drafting party and the adjudicating party in one
skill, blurring the separation Article 3 exists to keep. The fast loop already
faced this split and resolved it (ADR-0011): boot and close-out are two skills so
the close judges from fresh context.

## Options considered
### Option A — extend promotion-review with the reading-and-drafting procedure
Shape: one slow-loop skill holding both the agent's cross-pull-request drafting and
the human's adjudication ladder. Verdict: rejected — one skill spanning the
drafting party and the adjudicating party blurs Article 3's separation, and the
reader cannot tell which half is theirs.
### Option B — a separate consolidate skill for the agent pass, promotion-review kept as the human ladder
Shape: `consolidate` reads the fast-loop pull requests, walks the consolidation
lenses, routes each recurrence to its cheapest home, and drafts through
`corpus-write`; `promotion-review` keeps the bars and the ladder it cites.
Verdict: chosen — the split mirrors the fast loop's `session`/`close` division and
keeps each party's procedure in one home.

## Decision
The slow loop's agent-drafting half is the `consolidate` skill, distinct from the
human-run `promotion-review` skill and loaded from fresh context at a consolidation
pass. `consolidate` reads across the fast-loop pull requests for their signal —
dispositions and surviving lens answers — walks a fixed set of counterfactual-paired
consolidation lenses that each indict a contract slot, routes every ratified
recurrence to the cheapest existing authority before minting, and drafts the
residue as promotions through `corpus-write`. It proposes and never mints;
`promotion-review` holds the graded bars and the route-before-mint ladder, cited by
`consolidate`, never restated. Reading a pull request for its signal in the slow
loop is distinct from the fast-loop boot recovering learning from a pull request,
which ADR-0013 forecloses: the slow loop consumes deposited signal into a draft the
human still gates, and binds nothing.

## Consequences
Enables an agent-side consolidation procedure that judges accumulated signal from
fresh context without the human's adjudication instructions loading with it, and
keeps the bars in one home. Forecloses folding the agent's drafting pass into
`promotion-review`, and forecloses `consolidate` minting an entry or applying an
un-admitted candidate as knowledge.

## Warrant
- The consolidation procedure lives in `skills/consolidate/SKILL.md`; the graded
  bars and slot-signature table live in `skills/promotion-review/SKILL.md`, cited
  not copied (as of 2026-08-29).
- The consolidation lenses ship as angle-and-counterfactual pairs, per ADR-0012
  (skills/consolidate/SKILL.md).
- The slow loop consuming pull-request signal is distinguished from the boot
  learning from a pull request, which ADR-0013 forecloses
  (skills/consolidate/SKILL.md, "What this pass reads").
- False if: the agent's consolidation drafting is folded back into a single
  slow-loop skill, or `consolidate` is made to write to a store without a human
  merge.
