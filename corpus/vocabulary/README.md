# vocabulary/ — the shared-term register

**Kind:** register — terms corrected in place; git history carries the
amendments.

**Holds:** the tenant's grown extension of the corpus lexicon: names for
recurring work shapes, incident classes, and domain concepts that condition
fields latch on. A term here is a parse the producer of a condition and its
future consumer provably share — the register is the bridge the latch
language builds conditions from, and its class terms are the join keys that
group decisions, rules, and beliefs into generated per-class views.

**Live question (per term):** do producers and consumers still parse this
word the same way? A term is contradicted by divergence — two admitted
entries using it for different concepts — and the disposition of divergence
is a split into two re-anchored terms, never a vaguer shared definition.

**Reader and moment:** the author of any condition field, before using a
non-plain-English term; and any session for which a fired condition reads
ambiguously — the term's block is the tiebreak.

**Admission:** two independently anchored uses plus the human merge. A
candidate term arises where prose descriptions of one shape recur across
independent contexts; a term is never coined inside a condition, and a
taxonomy is never minted ahead of its instances.

**Retirement:** mootness — no condition or admitted entry references the
term. The reference count is derivable; the retirement itself, like every
retirement, is human.
