# beliefs/ — the world model

**Kind:** ledger. Claims are frozen verbatim at admission; a claim that needs
rewording is superseded, because contradiction against a drifted paraphrase
is contradiction against a strawman.

**Holds:** world-facing claims held as priced hypotheses: each entry a
claim, its falsifier, its deadline, and the reference it departs from —
frozen together in the admitting commit.

**Live question:** is this still true of the world? Entries are contradicted
by exogenous events — outcomes the project does not control — never by
internal preference.

**Reader and moment:** a session whose work depends on the claimed fact, at
the moment of dependence; and the adjudicating human when a deadline passes
or a falsifier fires. A fired falsifier makes the entry loud; it does not
retire it — disposition is human.

**Retirement:** settlement. A verdict (human-filled, or transcribed for
born-settled imports) flips status to `settled`; a partially settled claim is
superseded by narrowed successors. Settled entries remain as the record of
what was believed at what price.

**Imports:** a claim whose deadline predates its creation is a postdiction —
born settled at zero price, marked as such, and excluded from any skill
measure. Imported knowledge enters only through this shape; it never skips
the ladder into decisions or rules.
