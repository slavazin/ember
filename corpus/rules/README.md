# rules/ — the duty store

**Kind:** register, one per rule. A rule's hook, exclusions, and residue are
corrected in place as evidence accumulates; git carries the history. The
duty's *warrant* is not corrected here — it lives in the cited decisions.

**Holds:** recurring conditional duties — condition → obligation — routed to
sessions by work shape.

**Admission bar — the most gated store:** a rule requires as warrant at
least two admitted decisions observed on distinct surfaces, plus the human
merge. One recurrence is at most a decision; a duty generalized from a
single surface is a guess wearing a rule's authority. The bar exists because
every rule taxes every future session it routes to — cost that only
cross-surface evidence justifies.

**Live question:** is this duty discharged wherever it binds? A rule is
contradicted by the estate — work of the matching shape proceeding without
the duty — not by the world.

**Reader and moment:** sessions at plan time, through the generated index.
The index leads with each rule's residue and hook and never carries the duty
sentence — a duty obeyed from an index cell was obeyed without its warrant.

**Retirement:** demotion on mootness (the shape no longer occurs) or
coverage migration (a mechanical gate fully absorbs the duty, the rule
retiring to a pointer at the gate). Low firing frequency is never a
retirement reason — the rarely-firing rule can be the one that matters most.
